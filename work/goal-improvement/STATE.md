### Product brief and research
- **Observed/documented:** a focused English-language analytics application for Finnish Twitch streams. Live ranking, channel history, session viewer/chat activity, and community connections are its useful assets. Existing muted dark/violet styling, desktop/mobile web, Next/Hono/PostgreSQL, public/private visibility rules and ingestion boundaries govern the work.
- **Agent-selected hypothesis:** a returning viewer or broadcaster wants to find a known channel and understand its recent broadcasts even when it is offline. The only front-page search filters current live streams; six recently ended sessions and the chat-derived community map are unreliable routes to older/offline channels. Manually constructing `/channels/<login>` is the workaround. No user interviews, usage analytics, or demand claims were made.
- Success: starting from navigation or an unsuccessful live search, find an observed Finnish channel by name, see whether it is live, and reach its overview/history; preserve the search on back/reload/share and recover from API failure. Searching must never enumerate chatter-only or suppressed identities.
